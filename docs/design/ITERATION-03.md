# Studio 1.6.0 — visible card redesign

User feedback: the 1.5.9 cards still looked plain and did not communicate a premium visual identity. Spacing fixes alone did not address that problem.

The renderer now has six explicit art directions, including emerald with an inset ivory text plate, ivory editorial, charcoal photo gallery, a coral urgent bulletin, warm quotation stationery and a sage/emerald statistics card. Actual selected headline and body sizes remain unchanged.

A geometric lens emblem, actual page folio, measured masthead, metallic frame, asymmetric photo corner bars, tinted layered surfaces and a coordinated footer replace the shared flat stack. Decorative engraved arcs contain no data or fabricated reporting. Numeric headline emphasis remains in-place. All source image pixels remain visible in contain mode; text and image bounds stay separate.

Text pagination measures the exact label width, quote reserve and headline/body separator. Card surfaces are checked against the canvas and footer at every preset position. Transparent video overlays retain the selected surface and typography while leaving the rest transparent.

Native Android screenshots include the same synthetic sample used in 1.5.9, a six-template contact sheet and a direct before/after comparison. The comparison is intentionally based on the same source image and copy. Visual approval belongs to the user; successful geometry and rendering tests do not establish aesthetic approval.

Validation is provided by CI test reports, Android lint, APK assembly and inspection of the resulting native screenshots. Real-device video and Instagram delivery are not verified by this environment.
